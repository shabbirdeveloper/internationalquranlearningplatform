## Course catalogue

### Noorani Qaida and Quran Foundations

For beginners learning Arabic letters, sounds, joining rules, and the foundations of accurate Quran reading. The course builds confidence step by step through guided practice and regular revision.

**Best for:** children or adults beginning from the alphabet or rebuilding their reading foundation.

**Learning outcomes:**

- Recognize Arabic letters in their different forms.
- Produce letter sounds more accurately.
- Join letters and read short words.
- Understand introductory recitation rules.
- Prepare to read directly from the Quran.

### Quran Reading

Develop smoother, more confident Quran reading with individual correction of pronunciation, pace, stopping, and common reading mistakes.

**Best for:** learners who know the Arabic alphabet and want to read with greater independence.

### Quran with Tajweed

Learn the practical rules that support careful Quran recitation. Each rule is introduced clearly, demonstrated by the teacher, and applied during live reading.

**Best for:** readers who want more accurate pronunciation and disciplined recitation.

**Learning outcomes:**

- Improve articulation of Arabic letters.
- Apply rules of noon sakin, meem sakin, and elongation.
- Recognize appropriate stopping and starting points.
- Build consistency through correction and practice.

### Quran Memorization

Follow a structured memorization plan that balances new portions with daily revision and long-term retention. The learning pace is set around the student's capacity and available practice time.

**Best for:** learners seeking to memorize selected surahs or work toward a broader Hifz goal.

### Quran with Tafseer

Explore the meaning, context, themes, and guidance of selected Quranic passages with a teacher who can explain concepts at the learner's level.

**Best for:** teens and adults who want to understand what they recite and connect it with daily life.

### Ahlul Bayt and Islamic Studies

Build an age-appropriate understanding of beliefs, worship, ethics, Islamic history, and the teachings of the Prophet and Ahlul Bayt.

**Best for:** families seeking structured Shia Islamic learning alongside Quran study.

### Arabic Language

Develop foundational vocabulary, grammar, listening, and reading skills that support a deeper understanding of Quranic and Islamic texts.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who can join SHIA TALEEM?

Programs are intended for children, teens, and adults. The available course, teacher, and lesson format will depend on the learner's age, current level, goals, and schedule.

### Does a beginner need to know Arabic?

No. A complete beginner can start with Arabic letters, sounds, and joining rules before progressing to Quran reading.

### Can we request a male or female teacher?

Yes. You can share your preference during admission, and the team will consider it alongside language, course, schedule, and teacher availability.

### How long does a course take?

Progress varies by starting level, lesson frequency, age, learning goals, and practice between lessons. After assessment, the teacher can recommend a realistic learning path rather than promising a fixed completion date.

### How are lessons scheduled?

The academy reviews your time zone and weekly availability before confirming a recurring lesson time with the assigned teacher.

### How can parents follow progress?

Parents can receive visibility into schedules, attendance, teacher feedback, homework, and published progress updates through the family portal.

### What happens during the trial?

The teacher meets the learner, reviews the current level and goals, introduces the lesson format, and recommends appropriate next steps.
