# Pak Quran Academy content study

Source reviewed: <https://www.pakquranacademy.com/>

This document is a content and information-architecture study. It does not reproduce the source website's copy. The useful ideas are summarized so SHIA TALEEM can create original, accurate content.

## Public content structure found

### Main pages

- Home
- About
- Courses
  - Noorani Qaida
  - Quran recitation
  - Quran with Tajweed
  - Quran with Tafseer
  - Quran memorization
  - Islamic studies
- Teachers
  - Teacher overview
  - Male teachers
  - Female teachers
- Fee structure
- Contact and free-trial enquiry
- Blog
- Privacy policy

### Homepage conversion journey

The source website repeatedly presents a simple three-stage path:

1. Send an enquiry and share availability.
2. Attend trial lessons.
3. Select a monthly plan and complete admission.

This structure is useful because it answers the visitor's immediate question: “What happens after I contact the academy?”

## Recurring value propositions

- Live online Quran lessons from home.
- One-to-one instruction.
- Male and female teachers.
- Flexible scheduling for international learners.
- Courses for children and adults.
- Beginner-to-advanced pathways.
- Teacher communication skills, patience, and subject knowledge.
- Regular feedback and student progress reporting.
- A structured curriculum rather than disconnected lessons.
- A trial experience before paid enrolment.

## Course information pattern

Each course page generally contains:

- A learner-focused introduction.
- The intended audience and starting level.
- Learning outcomes.
- An indicative duration.
- Factors that affect progress, such as lesson frequency, age, class format, and home practice.
- A trial and admission call to action.
- Frequently asked questions.

## Teacher content pattern

The teacher section emphasizes:

- Quranic and Arabic subject knowledge.
- Teaching experience.
- Clear communication.
- Comfort with online teaching tools.
- Patience and compassion.
- Choice of male or female teacher.

SHIA TALEEM should only publish qualification or verification statements that are supported by its real approval process and records.

## Pricing pattern

The source groups plans by lessons per month and class length, then shows prices in several currencies. This is a clear model for comparison, but the prices should not be reused by SHIA TALEEM.

A suitable SHIA TALEEM pricing model would show:

- Lessons per month.
- Minutes per lesson.
- One-to-one or group format.
- What the plan includes.
- Billing currency and billing period.
- Rescheduling and cancellation terms.
- A clear admission action.

## FAQ themes worth covering

- Who can enrol?
- What ages are supported?
- Is prior Arabic knowledge required?
- Can families request a male or female teacher?
- How long is each lesson?
- How long does a course take?
- Can lesson times fit a child's school schedule?
- Which courses are available?
- How does the trial work?
- How do parents receive progress updates?

## Claims requiring SHIA TALEEM confirmation

Do not publish any of the following until the academy confirms the operational policy:

- Three days of free lessons.
- 24/7 teacher availability.
- A money-back guarantee.
- Course-completion certificates.
- Exact course duration.
- Exact age range.
- Monthly test reports.
- Specific class frequency or duration.
- Family discounts or reduced-fee programs.
- Any price or currency amount.
- “Certified”, “qualified”, or “verified” teacher claims without defined evidence.

## Content-quality observations

- The source has strong topic coverage and conversion repetition.
- Several pages repeat the same paragraphs, reducing clarity and credibility.
- Course duration statements are inconsistent in places.
- Some teacher and course copy appears on unrelated pages.
- SHIA TALEEM should use shorter, page-specific copy with one authoritative version of each policy.
